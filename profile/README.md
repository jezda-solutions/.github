# Jezda Solutions

Jezda Solutions is a software company in Smederevo, Serbia, established in 2018 by Zoran Jezdimirović. It helps European companies build and run their own dedicated engineering team in Serbia: developers employed by Jezda Solutions and managed day to day by the client. It also delivers nearshore custom software and business software products.

Website: [jezda.net](https://jezda.net/en/)

## Services

- **[Team Extension](https://jezda.net/en/solutions/team-extension/)**: developers in Serbia, junior to senior, employed by Jezda Solutions and managed day to day by the client.
- **[Subsidiary Setup](https://jezda.net/en/solutions/subsidiary-setup/)**: your own company in Serbia, 100% owned by you, which Jezda Solutions runs with you after setup.
- **[Hybrid Model](https://jezda.net/en/solutions/hybrid/)**: a staged path from outsourced team to owned Serbian office.
- **[Custom Software Development](https://jezda.net/en/solutions/custom-development/)**: nearshore custom web, mobile and enterprise software.

## Products

- **[ERP](https://jezda.net/en/products/erp/)**: modular business platform (HR, commerce, finance, projects).
- **[POS](https://jezda.net/en/products/pos/)**: fiscal-compliant point-of-sale software for retail and hospitality in Serbia.
- **[E-commerce](https://jezda.net/en/products/e-commerce/)**: custom webshops on a shared multi-tenant platform.
- **[Mobile Apps](https://jezda.net/en/products/mobile-apps/)**: Flutter, React Native, native and PWA development.
- **[AI Teams](https://jezda.net/en/products/ai-teams/)**: LLM integration, AI agents and RAG with an evaluation-first approach.
